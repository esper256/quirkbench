# Cricket sample

“Crickets Ambient Noise - loopable”, uploaded by Wolfgang_, optional attribution
Ted Kerr. CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/crickets-ambient-noise-loopable
Original: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/crickets_1.mp3

Converted to 48 kHz stereo signed 16-bit PCM. The last 50 ms overlaps the first
50 ms, with a linear crossfade; the overlapping tail is removed. Peak amplitude
is normalized to 0.8. Playback applies the chosen volume (default 0.1).
Conversion is performed once; target playback needs no decoder or network.
